# ABRM Safety, Education, and Claims Framework

ABRM is being built as a wellness and education platform. Product language should distinguish Matthew's observations, educational concepts, customer experiences, and claims supported by reliable evidence.

## Clearly present

- What ABRM is intended to teach
- How Matthew describes the method
- Demonstrations of movements/maneuvers
- Educational anatomy and movement concepts
- Personal experiences, clearly identified as personal experiences
- Published evidence and what it actually studied
- Wellness-oriented educational goals

## Do not casually present as established fact

- Claims that ABRM diagnoses or treats a disease
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Claims that a maneuver cures a medical condition
- Unsupported claims about biological processes
- Before/after claims presented as universally reproducible

## Demonstration safety

Each maneuver should have a place for appropriate cautions. Public demonstrations should not imply that every viewer should perform every movement.

## Privacy

The platform may handle accounts, progress, notes, purchases, and private-service inquiries. Collect only what is needed and protect it appropriately.

## Marketing claims workflow

1. State exactly what is being claimed.
2. Identify whether it is personal experience, customer-reported experience, expert interpretation, or research-supported evidence.
3. Find supporting sources for factual scientific claims.
4. Preserve uncertainty where evidence is limited.
5. Store approved wording in the claim/evidence record.

## Disclaimer

The final public disclaimer should be reviewed for the actual services, jurisdiction, business structure, and professional credentials before launch.
