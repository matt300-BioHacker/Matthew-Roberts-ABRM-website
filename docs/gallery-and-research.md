accessible enlarged-image dialogs, and an opt-in official TikTok creator embed.

is included. Photos are described as personal observations rather than controlled

collection. The official TikTok creator embed may show its latest video selection
when the visitor loads it, subject to availability.

Existing Amazon, Audible, Barnes & Noble, and Walmart destinations remain. The
Amazon audiobook button is explicitly a search destination; no guessed product
ASIN is represented as verified. Exact additional retailer links and a Books2Read
universal book link can be added when available.

/research/guide presents a structured framework with candidate mechanisms and
research questions. /research/brief introduces the author’s concise brief.
Complete supplied research-guide and academic-brief artifacts are hosted locally
under public/research-documents, preserving their document content and interactivity
without depending on expiring Meta/CDN URLs. External font imports were removed.
Unverified video-count language in the brief was replaced with channel references.

with a new research service category. No schema migration or recruitment workflow
was introduced. Kickstart remains unavailable.
