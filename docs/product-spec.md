# ABRM Platform Product Specification

## Core model

ABRM is a free public education platform with a premium layer.

### Public experience
- Understand ABRM and Matthew's story
- Read educational material
- Access the free guide
- Browse public maneuver education
- Watch YouTube/TikTok demonstrations
- Discover books
- Browse merchandise
- Join the email list
- Find the Facebook group
- Find the Skool community when available
- Learn about coaching and consultations
- See courses and programs

### Premium experience
- Premium courses
- Premium maneuver lessons
- Members-only videos/resources
- Structured learning paths
- Downloadable materials
- Progress tracking
- Member-only resources
- Early access to selected content

### Private services
Separate conversion paths for:
- Private 1-on-1 coaching
- Private consultations

These should support inquiry/application and scheduling workflows.

## Primary navigation

Home, What Is ABRM?, Learn, Maneuvers, Videos, Courses, Books, Merch, Coaching, Community, Research & Evidence, About Matthew, Contact.

Account/member controls should be separate from the main marketing navigation.

## Homepage funnel

Discover -> Learn -> Experience -> Go deeper -> Join

Sections:
- Hero and ABRM introduction
- What ABRM is
- Featured demonstration
- Free guide
- Featured education
- Books
- Merchandise
- Free and premium courses
- Coaching/consultation
- Community
- Email signup
- About Matthew
- Evidence/research positioning
- Legal/privacy/terms/disclaimer

## Monetization

Support:
- Free content
- Paid courses
- Premium membership
- Books
- Merchandise
- Private 1-on-1 coaching
- Private consultations
- Future digital products/programs

Use commerce integrations instead of recreating payment systems.

## Email funnel

Support free-guide delivery, newsletters, course launches, product announcements, new demonstration notifications, educational sequences, and consent/preferences.

Keep the email provider behind an integration boundary.

## Video and social

Treat YouTube and TikTok as discovery/content channels. A video record can store platform, URL, title, description, thumbnail, related maneuvers, related courses, and visibility.

Version one can link/embed existing videos.

## Community

Configurable destinations:
- Facebook group
- Skool community

If Skool is temporarily unavailable, the site can mark it unavailable without breaking the community page.

## Future AI

The future ABRM assistant should answer from the approved ABRM knowledge base rather than invent methodology. It should distinguish education from individualized professional advice and link to source ABRM content.

## V1 definition

1. Professional public website
2. ABRM methodology/story pages
3. Free guide + email capture
4. Maneuver/content library foundation
5. Video hub
6. Books and merchandise links
7. Free/premium course catalog
8. Premium access architecture
9. Coaching/consultation inquiry flow
10. Community links
11. Safety/claims framework
12. Analytics-ready architecture
